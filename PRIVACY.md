# Privacy Policy — For The King (draft)

Last updated: 2026-09-29

For The King (“the App”) is a discipleship companion for men’s huddles.

## Data we collect

- Account information (email, display name, auth provider identifiers)
- Huddle membership and invite-code joins
- Weekly checkmark progress (challenges, care for the body, journaling prompts)
- Journal notes you choose to save in the App: typed text and photos or scans of your journal pages, attached to journaling and SOAP items
- Huddle journaling settings chosen by the leader (whether notes are required, and whether notes are private or shared)
- Optional meeting dates for the huddle calendar
- Leader-selected group-challenge activity for certain weeks

## Data we do not collect

- Journal text or photos you don’t save in the App (writing in a paper journal stays on paper)
- Location, contacts, or health metrics beyond optional calendar event creation you initiate

## How we use data

To run huddles, show shared progress to brothers in the same huddle, keep the journal notes you save, and (if you choose) add meeting events to your device calendar.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Progress is visible only to members of your huddle.

Journal notes follow the huddle’s notes setting at the time you save them:

- **Private** (the default): only you can read the note and its photos. Your leader cannot read it.
- **Shared with huddle:** everyone in your huddle can read the note and its photos, from the Progress tab.

The notes screen always tells you which setting applies before you save. Changing the setting doesn’t change notes that were already saved; a note picks up the current setting the next time you edit it. Photos are stored in a private storage bucket and are only shown through short-lived links to people allowed to read the note.

## Deletion

You can delete your account from Settings → Delete account. This also deletes your journal notes and photos. Leaders who dissolve a huddle remove that huddle’s membership, progress, journal notes, and photos. You can delete an individual note by clearing its text and photos.
