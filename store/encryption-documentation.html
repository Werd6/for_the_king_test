Summary

Go and Make does not contain proprietary or non-standard encryption, and it does not include its own implementation of any standard encryption algorithm. All encryption in the app is provided by Apple's operating system. The app therefore qualifies for the exemption in Category 5, Part 2 of the U.S. Export Administration Regulations, and no encryption registration or classification is required.

How the app uses encryption

  • Network traffic (HTTPS/TLS). The app connects to its backend (Supabase) and to Expo's push notification service only over HTTPS, using iOS's built-in networking (URLSession). It adds no TLS library or custom transport encryption.
  • Saved sign-ins. Session tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain through Apple's Keychain Services (via the expo-secure-store library).
  • Hashing and random values. The app includes the expo-crypto library, which calls Apple's CommonCrypto for hashing and random numbers. It is not used to encrypt data.
  • Sign in with Apple and payments are handled entirely by Apple.

What the app does not contain

  • No proprietary or non-standard cryptographic algorithms.
  • No bundled cryptographic libraries (for example OpenSSL, libsodium or BoringSSL).
  • No end-to-end encryption, VPN, encrypted messaging or file encryption features.
